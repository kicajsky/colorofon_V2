# colorofon_V2
3-Channel Analog Colorofon

An analog color organ based on the MCP6002 operational amplifier, designed to visualize an audio signal through three independent frequency channels: Low, Mid, and High.

The circuit operates from a single 12 V supply and uses a 6 V virtual ground for analog signal processing. Each frequency channel independently processes the audio signal and provides a dedicated output for controlling external lighting.

3 frequency channels: Low / Mid / High
Fully analog signal processing
MCP6002 operational amplifier
12 V single-supply operation
6 V virtual ground
Audio signal input
Independent output for each channel
Schematic and PCB design included
